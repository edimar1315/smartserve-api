DOMAIN ENTITIES (8 Total)
===============================================================================

1. USER (Abstract Base Class)
   Fields:    Id, Name, Email, Phone, City, State, CreatedAt, UpdatedAt, IsActive
   Relations: Used by Professional and Client (TPH Inheritance)

2. PROFESSIONAL (Extends User)
   Fields:    AverageRating, TotalJobs, CPF, BankAccount, PixKey, IsVerified
              Latitude, Longitude, LastLocationUpdate
   Relations: Specializations (many-to-many), ProposalsCreated (one-to-many)

3. CLIENT (Extends User)
   Fields:    CPF, CreditCardToken, BillingAddress, BillingZipCode
              TotalSpent, CreditBalance, Latitude, Longitude
   Relations: ServiceRequests (one-to-many)

4. SPECIALIZATION
   Fields:    Id, Name, Description, Category, AveragePrice, IsActive, CreatedAt
   Relations: Professionals (many-to-many)

5. PROFESSIONALSPECIALIZATION (Junction Table)
   Fields:    Id, ProfessionalId, SpecializationId, HourlyRate
              YearsOfExperience, Certification, CreatedAt
   Relations: Professional (FK), Specialization (FK)

6. SERVICEREQUEST
   Fields:    Id, ClientId, SpecializationId, Title, Description, Address
              ZipCode, Latitude, Longitude, BudgetMin, BudgetMax, PreferredDate
              Status, ProposalCount, CreatedAt, CompletedAt
   Relations: Client (FK), Specialization (FK), Proposals (1-to-many), Payments (1-to-many)

7. PROPOSAL
   Fields:    Id, ServiceRequestId, ProfessionalId, ProposedPrice, Message
              EstimatedDays, Status, CreatedAt, UpdatedAt
   Relations: ServiceRequest (FK), Professional (FK)

8. PAYMENT
   Fields:    Id, ServiceRequestId, Amount, PaymentMethod, Status
              TransactionId, ReceiptUrl, CreatedAt, CompletedAt
   Relations: ServiceRequest (FK)
